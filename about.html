Who I am

Arlinda Kaloshi leads growth and marketing at Creative Hub Kosovo. She joined the organization in January 2022, moving from Digital Marketing Intern to Digital Marketing Specialist and, since June 2023, serving as Head of Growth & Marketing while acting as an academy mentor for learners.

In July 2025 she co-founded AdGate, bringing the same growth and marketing focus to a new venture. Her earlier experience spans social media management, advertising, and content creation at Mbresa Digital Agency.
