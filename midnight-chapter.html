How it works

Three simple steps, one good book a month.

01

Pick the book

Each month we choose one well-loved book — a mix of biography, story, and ideas. As a member, you get a say in the next pick.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you join.

How much time do I need each month? +

One book a month, read at your own pace — that's the whole commitment. Most of our picks take about 10–15 minutes a day on a commuting pace, and there are absolutely no quizzes, homework, or schedules to keep up with. The monthly chat is 60–75 minutes, and you're welcome to listen in even if you haven't finished the book.

How do the online discussions actually work? +

Every last Sunday of the month we meet over a live video call — camera optional, coffee encouraged. A week before, you'll get a short discussion guide with a handful of thoughtful questions, so nobody is ever put on the spot. If you can't make it live, a recording and the conversation notes are shared in the members' space the next morning.

What if I fall behind or skip a month? +

No pressure, ever. Fall behind and you simply catch up when you can — the chat is spoiler-friendly, and members happily steer around mid-book readers. Skip a whole month? That's completely fine too. The club is a habit you can lean on, not another obligation; you can participate as little or as much as life allows.
